Connect earbuds

常见问题FAQ

常见问题

Questions

真的免费吗？Is it really free?

是的，免费下载、没有广告、不用注册账号。

Yes — free to download, no ads and no account.

一定要戴耳机吗？Do I need earbuds?

不一定。不戴耳机时，同传的译文显示在屏幕上；也可以在「我的」里打开「全部用手机外放」。戴上耳机效果最好，译文在耳机里播报，也不容易漏识别。

No. Without earbuds, translations show on screen, or you can turn on “speaker for everything” in Settings. Earbuds give the best results.

没有网络能用吗？Does it work offline?

翻译在下载语言包后可以离线完成。语音识别能离线时在手机上完成，部分情况下需要联网由苹果处理。

Translation works offline after language packs are downloaded. Speech recognition runs on the device when possible and may use Apple's servers otherwise.

支持哪些手机？Which iPhones are supported?

需要 iOS 18 或更新版本的 iPhone。安卓和电脑可以先用网页版。

Any iPhone on iOS 18 or later. Android and desktop users can try the web version.
